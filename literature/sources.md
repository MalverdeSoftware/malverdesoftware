# Sources

All 114 works are in the Malverde central library under project MalverdeSoftware. The library ID is the stable reference; the citekey is the key in [the BibTeX file](../references/malverde-library.bib).

**Read** says what was actually checked. "Metadata only" means the work was not read. Status `needs-review` means its details come from a citing work or a thin record, so check them before citing; `inherited` means the details come from Zotero as entered.

## Historical evidence, name and identity

- **price2005** · `lit-089f2164ad64` · registry-verified
  Price, Patricia L. (2005). Of bandits and saints: Jesús Malverde and the struggle for place in Sinaloa, Mexico. *cultural geographies* 12(2): 175-197. SAGE Publications. [doi:10.1191/1474474005eu325oa](https://doi.org/10.1191/1474474005eu325oa)
  Central English study. Reports no archival evidence for a flesh-and-blood Malverde or Juárez Mazo (p. 191), and treats him as possibly legendary. No Indigenous, Yoreme or Mayo identity found. Notes that Malverde is not a Spanish surname (p. 194). **Read:** full text (HAL open copy; Firecrawl extraction, pp. 175, 176, 191, 194).
- **perea2020** · `lit-b38059fd4664` · source-verified
  Perea Romo, Diana María (2020). Jesús Malverde: el imaginario colectivo del bandido social y los exvotos en su capilla, 1909-2019. *Escripta. Revista de Historia* 2(4): 43-68. [Link](https://zenodo.org/records/15020495)
  Most careful archival treatment read. Gives the Paredones birth record (AHGES Libro 66, Acta 108, 15 Jan 1888). Finds it materially genuine, but it does not match the oral name or 1870 birth, and the child may have been named after the bandit. Attributes no Indigenous identity to him. **Read:** direct quotes from journal PDF via Firecrawl.
- **rocha2019** · `lit-bb1093e4b87b` · registry-verified

  Calls his existence hard to prove and the 1888 record not guaranteed authentic (p. 4). Uses the form Jesús Juárez Mazzo as an association in stories. Does not identify him as Yoreme, Mayo or Yaqui. **Read:** full text (Undermind PDF reader; pp. 3-5).
