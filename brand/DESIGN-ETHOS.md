# MalverdeSoftware design ethos

Chosen November 5, 2026. Direction B, the medal, from the sketches in `logo_v4/`.

## What the logo is for

The logo honors Jesús Malverde (Jesús Maaso), who was Yoreme/Yoeme. That is why the organization carries his name. MalverdeSoftware is Indigenous-led software development, and the mark should read as respect for a person, not as a mascot or a joke.

## The mark

A portrait of him inside a round medal.

- **Ring:** a green ring holds everything, like a devotional medal. It also gives the mark a ready shape for avatars and app icons.
- **Face:** flat-top hair, brows, downcast eyes and mustache, in dark ink on cream. The eyes stay. A recognizable likeness is the point.
- **Knot:** the neckerchief and its knot, in green. The tails run under the ring.

## Rules

1. **Green means him.** Only the ring and the knot are green. Everything else on the mark is ink or cream.
2. **Recognizable first.** If a change makes it less clearly him, it's the wrong change.
3. **Quiet expression.** Keep the downcast, composed gaze. No smile, no caricature, no props.
4. **Flat master, 3D treatment.** The flat vector is the logo. The Blender model is built from the same geometry and is used for large hero images. It is never the source.
5. **A small version is drawn, not shrunk.** Below 64px the eyes and fine details drop out and a separate simplified drawing takes over.
6. **Everything is generated by code.** Scripts in `logo_v4/` produce the SVG, PNG, icon and 3D files. Change the script, not the exports.
7. **Transparent backgrounds.** No background is baked into an export. Cream appears only as the medal's field.
8. **No added cultural motifs without the author's direction.** Yoeme imagery, including deer and flowers, is the author's decision and isn't decoration.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Green | `#3f8f1f` | Ring, knot |
| Ink | `#14260c` | Hair, brows, eyes, mustache |
| Cream | `#f4f3ee` | Medal field |

In 3D, the green uses the glossy gel from the earlier slime logo, which carries that logo forward. The hair and mustache are dark satin, and the field is glazed porcelain.

## 3D reference

Arca's visuals are the reference for depth and shading: the logo should look like a real, physically convincing object. They are a reference for quality only. The 3D logo is the logo by itself, with no scene, set or staging around it.

The 3D medal is one continuous sculpted relief, like a struck and enameled medal. It adds what a flat drawing can't: combed hair and mustache, pleated fabric, a dished field, a face modeled softly out of the cream, and a metal bezel around the ring. The neckerchief pieces touch in 3D, while the flat logo keeps its small gaps. The composition, proportions and colors stay the same as the flat logo.

## Name and wording

The name is one word in all UI and writing: **MalverdeSoftware**, never "Malverde Software". App footers read "MalverdeSoftware · Indigenous-led software development".

Each app keeps its own accent: green on Vote, blue on Pile. The logo stays green on both.

Apps are named with a lowercase "m" and one short word: **mVote** and **mPile**, and m___ for future apps. The rules are in the [UI spec](../docs/specs/SPEC-ui.md#app-names). The name doesn't change the mark, which stays his portrait.

## Source and rights

The reference portrait is AI-generated (kept with the project's private brand sources), and the author treats it as open to use. The hair and the right eye and brow are traced from it. The left eye and brow mirror the right. The mustache and knot are drawn by hand.
